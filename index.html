Safety

Safety and Important Considerations

“Natural” does not automatically mean suitable for everyone. Read this section before you buy.

General safety tips

  • Follow the label directions and never exceed the recommended serving.
  • Check the full ingredient list for anything you are allergic or sensitive to.
  • Some botanicals and nutrients can interact with prescription or over-the-counter medicines.
  • Stop use and contact a healthcare professional if you have an unwanted reaction.

See a clinician about symptoms like these

  • Blood in the urine
  • Pain or burning when urinating
  • Sudden changes in urinary habits
  • Persistent or worsening leakage

A supplement is not a substitute for evaluation of these symptoms.

Who may consider this product?

  • Adults looking for a daily nutritional routine focused on bladder and pelvic floor wellness
  • People who prefer one combined formula over several single-ingredient products
  • Those who have already discussed supplements with their healthcare provider

Who should consult a healthcare professional first?

  • Anyone who is pregnant, nursing, or planning pregnancy
  • Anyone taking prescription or over-the-counter medication
  • People with kidney, urinary, or other medical conditions
  • Anyone with persistent or unexplained urinary symptoms
  • Anyone scheduled for surgery
